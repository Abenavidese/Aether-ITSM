# Remote Access (VPN) Policy — NordPeak Corp

## Approved VPN client

NordPeak uses GlobalProtect only. The error ERR_VPN_809 at NordPeak means
your certificate expired: re-enroll it in the self-service portal.

## Access requests

VPN access at NordPeak is approved by the IT director and lasts 12 months.
Contractors never receive VPN access.

## Travel

VPN from abroad is allowed without prior notice at NordPeak.
