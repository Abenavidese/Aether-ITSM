CORE ECOMMERCE — EMPLOYEE / END-USER GUIDE
============================================

WHAT IS THIS
------------
Core Ecommerce is our internal shopping app: log in, browse products, add
them to a cart, and check out to generate an order. This guide covers the
normal flow and the most common problems employees run into.

HOW TO LOG IN
--------------
1. Go to the app's login page.
2. Use your email and password. If you don't have an account yet, click
   "Create an account" and register with any email + a password of at
   least 6 characters.
3. Sessions last 4 hours. After that you'll need to log in again — this is
   expected and is not an account lockout.

HOW TO BUY SOMETHING
----------------------
1. On the Shop page, products are grouped by category.
2. Click "Add to cart" on any in-stock item (out-of-stock items show
   "Out of stock" and the button is disabled).
3. Go to the Cart page to review items and the total.
4. Click "Facturar y comprar" to check out. This generates an order and
   clears your cart. You can review past orders any time under "Orders".

TROUBLESHOOTING (what to check before opening a ticket)
-----------------------------------------------------------
- "Invalid credentials" on login: double-check the email and password. If
  you don't remember creating an account, use "Create an account" instead
  of guessing a password.
- Logged out unexpectedly / "Invalid or expired token": normal after 4
  hours of inactivity or after switching devices. Just log in again.
- Can't add a product to the cart: it's out of stock. Ask an admin to check
  inventory — this is not something a normal account can fix.
- "Not enough stock" when checking out: someone else may have bought the
  remaining stock between you adding it to the cart and checking out.
  Reduce the quantity or try again later.
- "Cart is empty" when checking out: add at least one product before
  clicking "Facturar y comprar".
- Need to see your purchase history: go to "Orders" — it's per-account,
  admins can additionally see everyone's orders under "Admin".

WHO CAN DO WHAT
-----------------
- Customer accounts: browse, buy, and view their own order history.
- Admin accounts: everything a customer can do, plus editing product stock
  and viewing every order across all customers, under the "Admin" tab.
  Regular accounts cannot be upgraded to admin through the app itself —
  that's a manual, backend-side change.
